# Project Lakshmi — Weekly Analysis Report Format

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Canonical / Living  
**Established:** 9 September 2026  
**Canonical location:** `docs/project_lakshmi/weekly_analysis_report.md`  
**Initial habitat:** Nokku  
**Applies to:** Photosynthesis (PS), Nokku, and future Project Lakshmi consumers  
**Scope:** Shared methodology only; profile-neutral. Do not store any person's natal chart, personal numerology, preferred dates, BUY/SKIP history, or other profile-specific conclusions in this specification.

## 1. Purpose

Project Lakshmi may keep all detailed reasoning and explanation needed for auditability, but every weekly analysis must also end with the same concise mandatory report card.

The report format exists to prevent omissions, make PS and Nokku comparable, preserve evidence lineage, and allow future independent profiles/consumers to use the same method without inheriting another person's personal data or conclusions.

## 2. Canonical reasoning order

Use this order and keep the layers distinct:

**Government facts → statistical/historical evidence → personal numerology → natal astrology → current astrology → date/time-window synthesis → combined interpretation → decision**

Numerology and astrology are experimental/symbolic layers. They do not change the mathematical randomness of a lottery draw and must not be presented as a guaranteed-win mechanism.

## 3. Profile isolation rule

Before analysis, identify the Project Lakshmi profile being analysed and confirm that only that person's own profile inputs are being used.

Never inherit, infer, retrieve, or reuse another person's:

- personal numerology;
- natal placements or dasha;
- BUY/SKIP history;
- preferred numbers or dates;
- intuition records;
- lottery history;
- personal observations; or
- previous conclusions.

The shared methodology and Government/historical evidence may be reused when their evidence checkpoint remains valid.

## 4. Evidence freeze and current Government frontier

A prospective weekly experiment must record **two separate checkpoints** when applicable:

1. **Evidence freeze** — the last historical Government draw allowed to influence the prospective ranking. Normally this is the final published draw before the analysis week begins.
2. **Current Government frontier** — the latest canonical Government result currently preserved in Nokku.

Already-known results from inside the target week must not leak backward into the prospective ranking. They may later be used to evaluate the experiment.

Record at minimum:

- repository/branch/commit or equivalent evidence fingerprint;
- Government DrawSerial frontier;
- latest draw date;
- canonical record count;
- analysis week;
- draw calendar/status;
- all-prize-tier inclusion status; and
- known uncertainty or unavailable evidence.

If Government publication freshness is uncertain, say so. Do not silently substitute search-engine or cached evidence for the canonical Government/Collector path.

## 5. Required analytical sections

Detailed explanation may be as long as necessary, but the analysis should cover these sections when relevant.

### A. Government facts

- analysis week;
- evidence freeze;
- current canonical Government frontier;
- actionable vs already-past dates;
- lottery/draw code for each date;
- official draw time;
- official sale/purchase cutoff, if verified;
- any unavailable or unverified Government information.

### B. Historical statistical evidence

- sample definition and size;
- all-prize-tier inclusion confirmation;
- normalization rules;
- meaningful observed frequencies/recurrence;
- control/baseline where relevant;
- positive, neutral, or negative reading;
- limitations.

### C. Historical numerology signal

Keep observed historical frequency separate from numerological interpretation.

Use terms such as:

- historical alignment;
- observed association;
- selection signal; and
- experimental numerology observation.

Do not make a predictive-win claim.

### D. Personal numerology

For the current independent profile:

- fixed personal numbers used;
- date/day/full-date roots;
- personal day/month/year where applicable;
- draw-code root or other established Project Lakshmi numerology features;
- strongest and weakest dates;
- conflicts or mismatches.

### E. Natal astrology

- relevant natal placements;
- current dasha/background period where used;
- supportive and cautionary natal factors;
- limitations/uncertainty.

### F. Current astrology

For each relevant/actionable date:

- transit factors used;
- local date and timezone;
- key aspects/conjunctions/house relationships;
- supportive and cautionary factors;
- whether the reading is strong, moderate, mixed, weak, or cautionary.

### G. Time-window analysis — MANDATORY for BUY / SELECTIVE BUY

A recommendation is incomplete if it identifies a date but does not answer **when during that date the person should act**.

Keep these three time concepts separate:

1. **Official time** — Government-confirmed draw time and sale/purchase cutoff.
2. **Operational time** — a realistic period in which participation can actually occur before the cutoff.
3. **Symbolic time** — numerology/astrology-derived preferred and avoid periods within the day.

For every preferred/actionable date, record:

- timezone;
- primary preferred time window;
- backup time window, only if genuinely justified;
- avoid time window(s), if applicable;
- basis for each preferred/avoid window;
- relation to official draw/sale cutoff; and
- whether the window is Government-verified, operationally inferred, or symbolically derived.

Use explicit states when evidence is missing:

- **NOT VERIFIED** — official/cutoff information was not verified;
- **NO SYMBOLIC WINDOW ESTABLISHED** — analysis did not justify a symbolic window;
- **NOT APPLICABLE** — no participation is recommended.

Do not treat analysis of the Moon/transits at the draw time as automatically proving that the draw time itself is the best purchase time.

### H. Combined ranking

For each date, keep the layers visible rather than collapsing them too early:

- historical/statistical evidence;
- historical numerology;
- personal numerology;
- natal/current astrology;
- time-window quality;
- combined interpretation.

Past dates should be marked as historical/no action rather than recommended retrospectively.

## 6. PS and Nokku responsibility boundary

### Photosynthesis (PS)

PS may:

- consume the frozen/shared Government evidence;
- run the reusable statistical and historical numerology analysis;
- perform the agreed experimental personal/natal/current layers when the experiment calls for them;
- propose candidate dates and candidate time windows; and
- return an auditable experiment receipt.

PS must clearly label its ending as:

**PS Experimental Recommendation**

PS does not own the canonical Project Lakshmi decision.

### Nokku

Nokku independently reviews the PS evidence, verifies the profile-specific and current layers as needed, evaluates the proposed date **and time window**, resolves conflicts, and owns:

**Nokku Final Decision**

Canonical decision states may include:

- BUY;
- SELECTIVE BUY;
- OPTIONAL/BACKUP, when genuinely justified; or
- SKIP.

## 8. Minimum completeness gate

No final Project Lakshmi recommendation is complete without all six of these fields:

**Date + Lottery + Time Window + Evidence Freeze + Decision + Uncertainty**

For SKIP, the time-window field may be `NOT APPLICABLE`.

For BUY or SELECTIVE BUY, an actual actionable time window is mandatory; if it cannot be established, the report must say so rather than silently omitting it.

## 9. Participation discipline

A stronger symbolic signal does not justify escalating spend.

The report must state the intended experimental participation discipline, for example one selective participation or another explicitly bounded limit. Do not scatter participation across dates merely because several dates are available.

## 10. Experiment receipt / later evaluation

After the result becomes available, preserve the distinction between:

- what was known at decision time;
- what was predicted/ranked prospectively;
- what actually occurred; and
- what, if anything, the result teaches about the experimental methodology.

Do not rewrite the frozen weekly recommendation using hindsight.

## 11. Change control

This is a living specification. Change it only when real Project Lakshmi use exposes a genuine recurring gap or when shared methodology deliberately evolves.

Do not duplicate or fork the format casually. PS, Nokku, and future consumers should refer to this canonical file.

When a rule proves broadly reusable beyond Project Lakshmi/Nokku, consider promotion through the normal living-habitat/shared-evolution process rather than prematurely relocating the specification.
